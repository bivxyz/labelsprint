Help

Common LabelsPrint questions

Use these quick answers to choose a template, prepare label content, and export a finished sheet.

How do I choose a label template?

Use the Templates button in the app header to search by template number, size, category, shape, or labels per sheet.

Can I use mail merge data?

Yes. You can upload a CSV, paste rows or address blocks, or type label content manually.

What can I export?

LabelsPrint can download PDF or DOCX files and can also launch direct browser printing.
