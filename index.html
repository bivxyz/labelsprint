How it works

From your list to finished labels in three steps

LabelsPrint keeps the familiar mail merge workflow focused on the work that matters: add data, match a sheet, and print.

  1. 01

    Add your label content

    Type one label manually, paste multiple addresses, or upload a CSV exported from Excel or Google Sheets.

  2. 02

    Choose and customize

    Search Avery-compatible templates, format the text, and add an optional logo or built-in icon.

  3. 03

    Preview, download, or print

    Review the full sheet, edit individual labels, skip used positions, then print or export PDF and DOCX files.

Browser-based privacy

Your label list stays with you

LabelsPrint processes manual entries, pasted rows, CSV files, and uploaded logos locally in your browser. The web app does not require an account and does not send your label data to a LabelsPrint server.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you start printing

What is LabelsPrint?

LabelsPrint is a free browser-based label maker for creating and printing Avery-compatible label sheets.

Is LabelsPrint free?

Yes. The LabelsPrint web app is free to use and does not require an account or signup.

Can I make labels from Excel or Google Sheets data?

Yes. Export spreadsheet rows as a CSV and upload the file, or paste rows directly into LabelsPrint for a mail merge label workflow.

Which label templates does LabelsPrint support?

LabelsPrint includes a searchable catalog of calibrated Avery-compatible templates, including popular address, shipping, return, card, and badge formats.

What file formats can I download?

You can download finished label sheets as PDF or DOCX files, or print directly from your browser.

Does LabelsPrint upload my label data?

No. The web app processes label data locally in your browser and does not require an account.
